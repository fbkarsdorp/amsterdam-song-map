# Sound credits

The gulls and bells in the street are short excerpts of recordings on Wikimedia Commons.
Each was trimmed, high-pass filtered (to remove wind and traffic rumble), loudness
normalised, faded in and out, and converted to mono MP3. The open air is synthesised in
the browser (`src/sound.js`).
